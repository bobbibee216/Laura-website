About Laura

Laura Doss, LMT, graduated from the Mesa Academy of Massage in 2003 with a focus on clinical applications of massage. She has worked in chiropractic offices, hotel resorts, businesses, and private residences across Los Angeles and Orange County.

Over the years she stepped away from traditional massage to focus on craniosacral therapy and lymphatic drainage — gentle modalities that support less pain, more ease of movement, a calmer nervous system, and healthier lymph flow.

She sees clients by appointment in the South Bay, including at Wellness Den in Manhattan Beach.
